Anti-Heal Rule Early Game

Never contest sustain laners without Lifebane

Rush Sea Halberd (Attackers), Dominance Ice (Tanks/Fighters), or Glowing Wand (Mages) before 4 minutes against Estes, Yu Zhong, Ruby, or Uranus. Fighting them without anti-heal guarantees lane loss.

Magic Defense Rule Itemization

Know Burst vs Continuous Magic Reduction

Athena’s Shield provides 25% instant reduction for 3s against burst combos (Eudora, Kadita). Radiant Armor stacks magic defense up to 6 times against continuous multi-tick damage (Valir, Yve, Joy, Lunox).

Mobility Shutdown Drafting

Suppression and Grounding ignore Purify

High-mobility assassins (Fanny, Ling, Joy) can be stopped reliably only with Suppression (Franco, Kaja) or Grounding / Bounce (Minsitthar, Khufra). Standard stuns can be purified.
